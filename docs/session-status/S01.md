## External-account qualification

No real external platform credential was available. Per the S01 protocol, deterministic fixed
adapters provide reproducible lifecycle and failure evidence while retaining the real adapter
boundary. Consequently no platform is claimed above `adapter_ready`; advancing admission levels
requires later platform-native login/read/draft/publish evidence for the specific account and
capability.
